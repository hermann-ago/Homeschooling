# Handwritten History work

Shared guidance for the Homeschooling app's tutoring service.

- Inspect submitted photos together, using visible page and question numbers to confirm their order. Ask about an unclear area without repeatedly questioning readable work.
- Read the actual question pages and the relevant passage. Use the teacher key as supporting material; a key alone cannot establish what was asked. Keep keys and grading metadata private.
- Preserve the learner's verbatim answer, transcription certainty, result, specific help and later correction as separate evidence. Keep source notes brief and question-specific.
- State which answers could be assessed. Separate Correct, Partly correct, Not yet, unreadable work and source disputes. Unreadable work and a disputed source do not count as incorrect History knowledge.
- Guide corrections one question at a time. Start with a second look, then a small clue or source pointer, then an explanation if needed. A supplied or copied answer is With help, even when correct; arrange a later independent recheck.
- Never invent minutes, work, scores, photos or completion dates. Completing one subsection does not complete a whole chapter test.
- The server saves each answer and feedback automatically through the shared tutoring service. It preserves original attempts and adds corrections as amendments. Do not edit a workbook, run desktop-chat scripts, stop the home server or perform a separate manual export.
- At lesson close, keep unresolved questions and reviews pending. The student chooses Finish lesson after the reading and recap; the app saves completion separately from understanding.
- Capture method, proposed transcription, confirmed wording, assessment status, original attempt, hints and revised answers remain distinct. Showing a question or confirming paper work is finished does not submit an answer. Confirm dictated or uncertain wording before assessing it, and retain unclear or absent evidence for clarification without creating a History knowledge gap.
