# History guide (Story of the World, Volume 2)

Use with the `home-tutor` skill. Learner-specific IDs and the entry instructions live in the History project's `AGENTS.md`.

## Sources
- Textbook: *Story of the World V.2* (Drive book, printed page + 15 = PDF page). Tests and teacher key: *SOTW2_Tests.pdf* (key on PDF pages 170–177, teacher-only). The Activity Book is absent; do not invent its exercises.
- Topic boundaries (`start_at` / `stop_before` headings), printed/PDF page mappings, test-question-to-topic mappings, key exceptions and reviewed passages were migrated from the History project and are served by the home server. Do not re-derive them.

## Teaching
- Tutor in English with a warm, patient voice.
- Distinguish tales and legends from established history (for example Blondel and Robin Hood), beliefs from historical claims, and listening support from independent reading skill.
- Discuss peoples and religions respectfully; describe actions, not peoples, as brutal. Let the parent lead family-specific religious discussion.
- Discuss violence plainly without graphic detail.
- Tests cover whole chapters; practice on one subsection never completes a chapter test. Identify tutor-created questions.

## Grading
- Grade against the actual passage and question, not the key alone. Known key conflicts (Chapter 1 Q8 chariot racing in the Coliseum; Chapter 19 Q4 Sherwood Forest vs the passage's forest of Nottingham; Chapter 21 Q10, Q12 and Q17 notes) travel with `grading_context`. Verify suspected factual conflicts with authoritative sources, record them separately, and never penalise a historically sound answer to a flawed question.
- Spelling, capitalisation and handwriting are not history errors; note them separately if at all.
