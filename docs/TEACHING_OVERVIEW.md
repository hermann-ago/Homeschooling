# How teaching and learning records work

Updated against the app and Lucas's saved records on 8 October 2026. Integrated rollout still requires parent inspection.

## From books to a lesson

As of 8 October, **Curriculum → Books & lessons → Books to understand together** saves a versioned subject collection. **Add books** assembles existing PDFs or staged uploads without starting AI generation. **Organize books** sets the main reading book, source order, edition labels, companion textbooks, and exact answer-key targets. Saving a changed collection creates an immutable membership snapshot; removing a collection member keeps its existing prepared materials and learner records. Conflicting edits from another device are refused until the collection is reopened.

**Understand these books together** now opens a free preview of reused scans, unfinished analysis, and estimated cost. A parent selects a total job maximum before starting. Background preparation saves section summaries, page evidence, dated events, exercise connections, and an overview across the collection. Requests must fit both that maximum and the monthly book allowance. **Pause review** and **Continue preparation** retain completed work; uncertain sent requests remain reserved. **View saved overview** reopens the result without generation. Relationships are proposals for parent review. The detailed map lets parents inspect original sources, correct proposals and explicitly select checked portions for new lessons. Started lessons keep their previous context.

Existing **Prepare book**, **Improve remaining links**, and **Retry remaining work** actions still explicitly start individual preparation and use the family allowance. The collection review does not rewrite prepared exercises, started lessons, guides, recordings, or learner records. In test mode, collection edits and analysis jobs stay in the private working area, while exact-input paid responses remain reusable through the shared cache.

The overview is saved before all exercise links finish. If the job pauses at its spending limit, **View saved overview** still works and labels the pending links. Source-validation issues and unusable bounded replies remain review items; they do not become accepted facts or trigger automatic repeat purchases. The source map and correction tools are available without further generation.

1. A textbook supplies the reading sections and their exact page boundaries.
2. A workbook or test booklet supplies the original numbered exercises. Preparation saves small batches so valid batches can be reused after interruptions.
3. The app connects an exercise to the reading needed to answer it. New AI matches need supporting quotations from that reading; uncertain matches wait for parent review. Previously reviewed History mappings and key exceptions are preserved.
4. A saved subject teaching skill sets the teaching style and assessment rules. The lesson prompt also receives learner preferences, the unfinished lesson checkpoint, the two most recent completed lessons, due reviews, pending work and relevant older evidence from the lasting learning summary. Explicitly checked map connections and original earlier passages can support continuity.
5. A guide adds short explanations and activities around the reading. For ordinary textbook passages, the server inserts the original words in order. Teacher scripts use a separate rewritten delivery mode that needs source review.
6. Eligible unfinished core exercises are added after the required reading. Exercises needing a future reading section wait. Optional work is offered separately.

Source validation checks coverage, numbering and evidence. It does not prove that every explanation or assessment is correct; parents can review sources and correct saved transcriptions.

## Supported book arrangements

| Arrangement | Current support |
|---|---|
| Textbook alone | Guided reading and AI understanding checks. |
| Separate textbook and workbook/test booklet | Explicit companion links and an individual exercise catalog. |
| History textbook, tests and answer key | Reviewed History key entries and exceptions are used for the relevant questions. |
| Newly uploaded separate answer key | Choose its exact workbook/edition. Individual entries require original question identity and a supporting source quotation. Conflicting or unreadable entries wait for review. |
| One book containing both teaching and exercises | Choose **Textbook + workbook**. Preparation creates both reading topics and numbered exercises. Only quoted student reading blocks enter the reader; teacher pages and raw combined PDFs stay out of the student lesson. |

Each question now contributes its own reading candidates, including sections from its chapter. The matcher can request one bounded follow-up with a missing passage from the outline. Accepted links, parent corrections and individual matching results are reused. A failed request or spending pause saves completed results and leaves an already prepared workbook usable. **Improve remaining links** processes saved scans; **Retry remaining work** resumes a paused job. Unresolved proposals remain visible for source review instead of being regenerated repeatedly.

Separate keys are linked by the selected book, source edition, chapter and original question reference. Repeated numbers also require an unambiguous section. Changing a key's target creates new exercise versions; started assignments retain their original snapshots. Combined-book reading still needs parent source review before narration. These new arrangements have been tested with isolated fixtures; each family's new book should be checked before student use.

## What the app records

- The learner's first answer, its assessment, the help supplied and any later answer.
- Independent understanding separately from a correct answer given with help.
- Submitted photos, transcriptions, feedback and references to the exercise version used.
- Pending or skipped activities, unfinished lesson checkpoints and the exact next prompt.
- Specific concepts to revisit, the evidence for them and a review due date.
- Lesson completion separately from understanding. Listening or checking off reading does not establish mastery.

Unreadable handwriting or disputed sources require clarification instead of a wrong mark. A corrected transcription preserves the original as an amendment.

In integrated lessons, partially correct answers, incorrect answers and correct answers with help create or update review records. Due reviews and unfinished activities are brought into later lessons. A review closes after a later independent check. This is a record of observed difficulties; it is not yet a comprehensive map of every skill a child has mastered.

Only work submitted or deliberately recorded in the app is included. Work done on paper or in another conversation is not automatically known to the app.

## Stable lessons, changing recall

New guide preparation is PDF-first. It attaches original assigned pages and linked exercise pages alongside exact extracted text, reusable teaching skills and current learner context. Review inspects those same pages and any explicitly mapped private keys. Partial-page boundaries remain binding; mixed teacher/student sources retain checked student text. An explicit `lesson_source: text` teaching-skill setting preserves text-only preparation. Full-book JSON companions are optional supporting indexes. Assigned pages are limited to 20, combined source bundles to 40 pages and 20 MB. Exact PDFs and page maps are frozen with new guides; existing lessons remain pinned. See [PDF-first preparation and cleanup ledger](PDF_FIRST_PREPARATION.md) for compatibility, verification and removal candidates.

Automatic narration is durably queued and launched independently of lesson responses. Provider delays or quota backoff leave creation/start available, retain completed audio parts and preserve existing purchase reservations. Missing recordings remain visible as preparing or text-only until a saved recording is available.

Completed source analysis is also saved in a standardized **Subject Knowledge** JSON file inside the subject folder. **Use saved subject knowledge** assigns an already analyzed family collection to another child without a new analysis or copying learner work. PDF fingerprints are checked; original files must remain available. The child receives their own source selection, personalized guides, attempts and learning history. Reading topics are assigned separately. Parent source corrections apply to the shared books; changing one subject's book list creates its own collection. See `SUBJECT_KNOWLEDGE_FORMAT.md` for the archive format and recovery boundaries.

Guided reading previews stop at the saved student activities and provide **Start answering** or **Continue preview without answering**. Previewing saves no learner evidence. Starting an interactive lesson opens typing/photo input; reading stays paused through assessment and adaptive follow-up questions until an explicit continue. If another real lesson is unfinished, its resume link remains available and the new topic can only be previewed. New guide prompts require questions inviting a response to be structured activities, rather than hidden inside explanations. Existing saved scripts and recordings are not automatically rewritten.

Progress includes a learning summary for each child and subject, with supporting observations, independence/help, later checks and older open reviews. Unknown legacy concept associations remain unknown until a parent links them deliberately. Photos and amendments remain inspectable; a correction preserves the original record and assistance history.

Curriculum offers **Prepare upcoming lessons** for one or two lessons with a free cost preview and a maximum per guide. Current learning evidence can add one separate recall task before a saved lesson. The main guide and its recordings remain stable. Existing sessions resume at the exact checkpoint with their pinned guide and source selection.

Recordings reuse identical text across guide versions and save completed passages individually. A changed introduction need not repurchase the book reading. Read aloud on saved feedback and recall tasks retrieves their stored text. Passage highlighting, activity stops, speed controls and device speech remain available. Test-mode work does not alter real learning summaries; its exact audio can be reused later.
